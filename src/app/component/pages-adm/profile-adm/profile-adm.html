Help & Support Guides and frequently asked questions

How do I update my profile?

Close Settings, select Edit Information in My Profile, make your changes, and select Save Changes.

How do I change my password?

Open Change Password and enter your current password, new password, and confirmation.

Where can I manage customer orders?

Select Customer Orders from the profile sidebar or Staff Quick Access.

For account or permission issues, contact your designated SilkMart system administrator.
