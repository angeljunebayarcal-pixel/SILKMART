Help & Support Information about using SilkMart

How do I place an order?

Open Shop Silk, choose an available product, and proceed through the ordering process.

How do I request a delivery date?

Enter your preferred delivery date when placing an order. Staff will review the request.

Where can I check my orders?

Open My Orders to see your submitted orders and their current statuses.

Can I pay online?

SilkMart currently supports Cash on Delivery (COD). Online payment is not included.

How do I update my address?

Close Settings, select Edit Information, update your address, and save your changes. Check the delivery information when placing an order.

For concerns about orders or delivery arrangements, contact the authorized SilkMart staff.

About SilkMart Learn about the SilkMart system

Application

SilkMart — VILLANUEVA (DOST-PTRI)

Purpose

SilkMart is a customer ordering and staff management system for silkworm products and related inventory.

Customer Features

Browse products, place orders, request preferred delivery dates, review order statuses, and view notifications.

Staff Functions

Manage product listings, silkworm inventory, cocoon records, customer orders, and delivery arrangements.

Payment Method

Cash on Delivery (COD)

SilkMart does not provide live courier tracking or automated delivery dispatch.
